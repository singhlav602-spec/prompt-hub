How it works

  1. Choose a design mode. Add a headline only when you want text on the design; No Text mode works for wallpapers and clean backgrounds.
  2. Choose a target canvas, background and text colors, then customize composition, negative space and lighting.
  3. When text is enabled, choose typography and effects, then check the contrast note under the preview.
  4. Press Generate prompt, copy it, and paste it into your AI image tool.

Questions

Does this tool create the poster image?

No. The live preview shows your selected text and colors. This tool writes a detailed prompt for an AI image tool such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Ideogram or Midjourney; it does not render the final artwork itself.

Can I use two colors mixed together for the text?

Yes. Switch the text color to Mixed colors, choose two colors, and the preview and the prompt will both use the blend. The background can be a gradient too.

Why does the AI sometimes misspell my headline?

Image models can still slip on lettering. The prompt tells the AI to spell every letter exactly, and tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Ideogram handle text best. If a letter is wrong, ask the AI to fix the spelling and keep everything else the same.

Is the poster prompt generator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser.
