About SmartPrompts

What SmartPrompts is

SmartPrompts is a free library of AI prompts: ready-made instructions you can copy, fill in and paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek or any other AI chat tool. It is an independent project, and it now holds thousands of prompts across more than a hundred categories, from writing, coding and marketing to education, business, YouTube and AI image and video creation.

Why we made it

A good prompt can be the difference between a vague AI answer and a useful one, but writing good prompts takes practice. We wanted a place where anyone can find a solid starting point for the task in front of them, without paying for it and without creating an account. You can browse, search and copy everything on the site for free.

How a prompt gets here

Prompts are written and organised by us, and some are first drafted with the help of an AI tool. Nothing is added automatically: every prompt is read and edited before it is saved to the library. Prompts sent in through the Submit Prompt page go into a review queue, and we check each one by hand before it goes live. The articles on the Blog are written by us as well.

How to get the most from a prompt

Treat every prompt as a starting point. Replace the highlighted placeholders with your own details, because specific details are what make an answer useful. Then read the AI's reply critically and ask follow-up questions to refine it. AI tools can make mistakes, so check important facts before you rely on them.

Free tools

Besides the library, you can use our free tools. The Prompt Improver rewrites a rough prompt so it gets better results, the Image Prompt Generator turns a simple idea into a detailed picture prompt, and the SEO Title & Meta Description Generator writes search-friendly page titles.

Something wrong? Tell us

If a prompt does not work, contains a mistake or does not belong in the library, use the "Report it" link at the bottom of that prompt's page, or write to us from the Contact page. We read every message and reply as soon as we can. You can also read how we handle your data in our Privacy Policy.

Stay updated

New prompts and articles are added regularly. Check the Blog for the latest, or subscribe from the homepage.
