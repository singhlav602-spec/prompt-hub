About SmartPrompts

What this is

SmartPrompts is a free, growing library of hand-crafted AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and DeepSeek. Every prompt is ready to copy and paste — fill in the bracketed placeholders and go.

Why it's free

Good prompts shouldn't be locked behind a paywall. The whole library is browsable and copyable with no account required.

Community-driven

Anyone can suggest a new prompt from the Submit Prompt page. Every submission is reviewed by hand before it goes live, to keep the library useful and spam-free.

Stay updated

New prompts and articles are added regularly — check the Blog for the latest, or subscribe from the homepage.
