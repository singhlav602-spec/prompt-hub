Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information SmartPrompts collects when you visit smart-prompt.in, how it's used, and the choices you have. Browsing and using the prompt library doesn't require an account, and we don't ask for any personal information to search, copy, or use a prompt.

Information We Collect

We collect two kinds of information:

Automatically, via analytics: like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally.

Information you provide directly: if you use the Submit a Prompt page, we store whatever you enter in that form (the prompt content and any name or contact detail you choose to include) so we can review it before publishing.

Cookies & Local Storage

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. Separately, the site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember things like your saved/liked prompts and light/dark theme preference — this data stays on your device and isn't sent to our servers.

Advertising

SmartPrompts may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. You can read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Links

Some pages link out to third-party AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others) or other external sites. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices or content of any site we don't operate — check their own policies before sharing information with them.

Children's Privacy

SmartPrompts isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can clear your saved/liked prompts and theme preference at any time by clearing your browser's site data for smart-prompt.in. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads using the links above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy. Check the "last updated" date above.
